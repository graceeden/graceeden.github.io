<section class="page"><div class="wrap"><div class="head"><div class="label">Research</div><div><h1>Human-centred inquiry into emerging technologies.</h1><p class="intro">My research sits at the intersection of Human-Centred AI, Design Futures and Human–Computer Interaction. I study how people interpret, negotiate and shape technologies as they enter everyday, organisational and institutional settings.</p></div></div>
<div class="three"><article class="panel"><span class="kicker">01 · Human-Centred AI</span><h3>People working with increasingly capable AI</h3><p>Research on generative AI, autonomous systems and AI-supported work, with attention to interpretation, trust, accountability and the changing nature of human judgement.</p></article><article class="panel"><span class="kicker">02 · Design Futures</span><h3>Exploring alternative technological futures</h3><p>Speculative design, design fiction and participatory approaches are used to make possible futures discussable and to examine assumptions embedded in emerging technologies.</p></article><article class="panel"><span class="kicker">03 · Interaction & Society</span><h3>Technology in lived and institutional contexts</h3><p>Empirical HCI research examines interaction in domains including mobility, education, privacy and digital innovation.</p></article></div>
<div class="section" style="padding-left:0;padding-right:0;border-bottom:0"><div class="head"><div class="label">Research trajectory</div><div><h2>From interaction design to Human-Centred AI.</h2><p class="intro">My work has developed from usability engineering and interaction design into empirical studies of sociotechnical systems and design-led exploration of technological futures. The common thread is an interest in what people actually do, how technologies become meaningful in practice, and how design can open up better possibilities.</p></div></div></div></div></section>
