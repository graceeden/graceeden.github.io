I moved into academic research and teaching through roles at the University of Oxford, HES-SO Valais-Wallis, Manchester Metropolitan University, IIIT-Delhi, the University of York and the University of Tartu.</p><p>This trajectory continues to shape my research: I combine practical experience of designing and evaluating interactive systems with empirical investigation of how technologies are used and understood.</p></div><div class="panel"><h3>Current focus</h3><p>My current work examines Human-Centred AI, including generative AI and autonomous systems, alongside Design Futures and empirical HCI.</p><p>I am interested in technologies as sociotechnical systems: not simply what a system can do, but how people interpret it, incorporate it into practice, contest it and imagine alternatives.</p></div></div></div></section>
