Research

Designing better relationships between people and technology.

My research brings together Human-Centred AI, Design Futures and empirical Human–Computer Interaction. I am interested in what people actually do with technologies, how systems become meaningful in practice, and how design can help us imagine and build alternatives.

Selected work
01 / Mobility

Autonomous vehicles

Understanding how pedestrians and other road users interpret autonomous systems and their behaviour.

02 / Futures

Designing possible futures

Using speculative design and design fiction to make assumptions about emerging technologies visible and discussable.

03 / Human–AI

People working with AI

Exploring interpretation, judgement, trust and accountability as increasingly capable AI enters everyday and organisational settings.

04 / Education

Technology in lived contexts

Empirical research into education, digital innovation, privacy and the institutional realities surrounding technology.

About

Researcher, educator and academic leader.

My career spans academia and industry, with more than two decades working across interaction design, usability, HCI, design innovation and emerging technologies. I hold a PhD from the University of Oxford and have worked in India, the UK, Switzerland and Estonia.

Across these contexts, my work is grounded in empirical inquiry and a commitment to making technology more accountable to the people and societies in which it operates.
